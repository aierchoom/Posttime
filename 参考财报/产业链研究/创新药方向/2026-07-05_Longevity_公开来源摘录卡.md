# Longevity / 衰老干预公开来源摘录卡

> 说明：本卡记录本次观点更新使用的公开来源、访问日期、来源层级和关键事实。部分公司为私营 biotech，融资、管线和估值信息透明度低；本卡将其作为方向热度和产业映射信号，不作为单家公司估值依据。

## U1 WHO Ageing and health

- URL: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/ageing-and-health
- Accessed: 2026-07-05
- Source tier: Tier 1 / WHO
- Key facts:
  - WHO fact sheet states that in 2020 the number of people aged 60 years and older outnumbered children younger than 5 years.
  - WHO states the population aged 60 years and older is expected to reach 1.4 billion in 2030 and 2.1 billion in 2050.
  - Use in report: demographic demand base for healthspan and age-related chronic disease R&D.

## U2 Hallmarks of aging: An expanding universe

- URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001
- Publisher: Cell
- Published: 2023
- Accessed: 2026-07-05
- Source tier: Tier 1B / peer-reviewed review
- Key facts:
  - The review expands and systematizes hallmarks-of-aging biology, including molecular damage, deregulated nutrient sensing, mitochondrial dysfunction, cellular senescence, stem cell exhaustion, altered intercellular communication, chronic inflammation and related mechanisms.
  - Use in report: supports that longevity can be decomposed into druggable biological hypotheses, but is not clinical efficacy evidence.

## U3 AFAR TAME Trial

- URL: https://www.afar.org/tame-trial
- Accessed: 2026-07-05
- Source tier: Tier 1B / nonprofit geroscience program page
- Key facts:
  - TAME is described as a planned clinical trial testing whether metformin can delay the development or progression of age-related chronic diseases and conditions.
  - AFAR describes the trial as involving approximately 3,000 people aged 65-79 and about 6 years of follow-up.
  - Use in report: endpoint-design and regulatory-pathway signal; not treated as completed positive efficacy evidence.
