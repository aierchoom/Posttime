TTM Technologies, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

October 22, 2026 6:00am EDT Download as PDF

Related Documents

Earnings Webcast Audio
Earnings Presentation PDF

SANTA ANA, Calif., Oct. 22, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TTM Technologies, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTMI) (“TTM”), a leading global manufacturer of technology products, including mission systems, radio frequency (“RF”) components, RF microwave/microelectronic assemblies, and technologically advanced interconnect products, including printed circuit boards (“PCB”s) and substrates, today reported results for the third quarter of 2026, which ended on September 28, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

  • Net sales were $1.2 billion, up 60% year on year, and an all-time quarterly record
  • GAAP net income of $84.8 million, or $0.79 per diluted share
  • Adjusted EBITDA of $242.3 million, or 20.1% of net sales, an all-time quarterly record
  • Non-GAAP net income of $157.8 million, or $1.46 per diluted share, an all-time quarterly record
  • Cash flow from operations of $154.7 million, or 12.9% of net sales
  • Total book to bill of 2.13
  • A&D end market was 34% of total net sales; and total A&D program backlog was $2.0 billion
  • Data Center and Networking end market was 41% of total net sales driven by continued AI demand

Recent Acquisition Activity

  • October 19, 2026 – TTM announced plans to acquire the Ophir Optics business from MKS Inc. (NASDAQ: MKSI) for $650 million, or 10.3x expected FY 2027 Adjusted EBITDA, in an all-cash transaction that is expected to be funded through a combination of cash on hand, expected future cash flow, and borrowings under TTM’s existing revolving credit facility. This proposed acquisition is not included in current guidance.

  • October 9, 2026 – In furtherance of TTM’s strategic initiative to expand its footprint in Europe, TTM entered into a definitive agreement with plans to acquire Merlin Circuit Technology Ltd. and Merlin Flex Ltd., two small printed circuit board fabrication factories in the United Kingdom for an undisclosed amount, with transaction funding expected to come from cash on hand. The transaction is subject to regulatory approvals and is expected to close during the fourth quarter of 2026. This proposed acquisition is not included in current guidance.

  • TTM previously announced the planned acquisition of ILFA GmbH, a German printed circuit manufacturer. The acquisition is expected to close during the fourth quarter of 2026 and is excluded from current guidance.

  • In recent weeks and up through the date of this press release, TTM has completed the previously announced acquisitions of Swiss Technology Group (“STG”) and Epiq Solutions (“Epiq”). These acquisitions are now included in current guidance.

Third Quarter 2026 GAAP Financial Results

Net sales in the third quarter of 2026 were $1.2 billion, compared to $752.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.

GAAP operating income in the third quarter of 2026 was $162.3 million. This compared to GAAP operating income in the third quarter of 2025 of $71.9 million.

GAAP net income in the third quarter of 2026 was $84.8 million, or $0.79 per diluted share. This compared to GAAP net income in the third quarter of 2025 of $53.1 million, or $0.50 per diluted share.

Third Quarter 2026 Non-GAAP Financial Results

Adjusted EBITDA in the third quarter of 2026 was $242.3 million, or 20.1% of net sales, compared to adjusted EBITDA of $120.9 million, or 16.1% of net sales, in the third quarter of 2025.

Non-GAAP net income in the third quarter of 2026 was $157.8 million, or $1.46 per diluted share, compared to non-GAAP net income of $71.0 million, or $0.67 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025.

“Once again, we delivered quarterly records for both net sales and non-GAAP EPS,” said Edwin Roks, CEO of TTM. “All key markets contributed to our 60% year on year growth with Data Center & Networking up 115%, Medical, Industrial & Instrumentation up 97%, and Aerospace & Defense up 21%. Our margin expansion efforts are producing results as evidenced by adjusted EBITDA margin exceeding 20% for the first time. Our cash flow from operations continues to be solid which gives us confidence that we can comfortably handle the leverage from recent acquisitions and capital investments. Following the end of the third quarter and the previously disclosed completion of the Epiq Solutions acquisition on September 30, 2026, our net leverage ratio as of September 30, 2026, was 2.8x of pro-forma adjusted TTM EBITDA. By the expected closing date of the proposed acquisition of the Ophir Optics business, we expect leverage to be within our targeted range of 1.5-2.0x,” concluded Dr. Roks.

Dr. Roks added, “TTM is well positioned for growth for the foreseeable future. We expect continued healthy growth in our key end markets, with particular strength in Data Center & Networking and Aerospace & Defense. Our close customer relationships and strong operational execution add to our confidence in this outlook. Consistent with our strategic plan, the recent acquisitions broaden our offering in differentiated, long-cycle products, add geographic diversification and provide new platforms for future growth."

Business Outlook

For the fourth quarter of 2026, TTM estimates that net sales will be in the range of $1.42 billion to $1.44 billion, and non-GAAP net income will be in the range of $1.77 to $1.83 per diluted share. These fourth quarter estimates result in full year 2026 expected net sales of $4.47 billion to $4.49 billion and expected non-GAAP net income in the range of $4.97 to $5.03 per diluted share. Our fourth quarter estimate and full year 2026 outlook now include contributions from our closed STG and Epiq acquisition and associated financing costs.

In addition, we are initiating our preliminary 2027 full year forecast with expected net sales in the range of $6.5 billion to $6.75 billion and expected non-GAAP net income in the range of $8.10 to $8.90 per diluted share. Our initial 2027 full year forecast includes contributions from recently closed acquisitions and associated financing costs while excluding the impacts of planned or potential acquisitions that have not been closed.

With respect to TTM’s outlook for non-GAAP net income per diluted share, we are unable to predict with reasonable certainty or without unreasonable effort certain items that may affect a comparable measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. Our expected non-GAAP net income per diluted share excludes primarily the future impact of restructuring actions, impairment charges, unusual gains and losses including but not limited to unrealized foreign exchange translation, and tax adjustments. These reconciling items are highly variable and difficult to predict due to various factors outside of management’s control and could have a material impact on our future period net income per diluted share calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. Accordingly, a reconciliation of non-GAAP net income per diluted share to a comparable measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP has not been provided because TTM is unable to provide such reconciliation without unreasonable effort. For the same reasons, TTM is unable to address the probable significance of the information.

About TTM

TTM Technologies, Inc. is a leading global manufacturer of technology products, including mission systems, radio frequency (“RF”) components, RF microwave/microelectronic assemblies, and technologically advanced interconnect products, including PCBs and substrates. TTM stands for time-to-market, representing how TTM's time-critical, one-stop design, engineering and manufacturing services enable customers to reduce the time required to develop new products and bring them to market. Additional information can be found at www.ttm.com.

  7 Adjusted EBITDA is defined as earnings before income tax (benefit) provision, interest expense, amortization of definite-lived intangibles, depreciation expense, stock-based compensation, loss on extinguishment of debt, unrealized (gain) loss on commodity hedge, unrealized (gain) loss on foreign exchange, realized and unrealized losses on derivative instruments, restructuring, acquisition-related, and other charges. We present adjusted EBITDA to enhance the understanding of our operating results, and it is a key measure we use to evaluate our operations. In addition, we provide our adjusted EBITDA because we believe that investors and securities analysts will find adjusted EBITDA to be a useful measure for evaluating our operating performance and comparing our operating performance with that of similar companies that have different capital structures and for evaluating our ability to meet our future debt service, capital expenditures, and working capital requirements.
